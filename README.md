# 🎯 Achievr

**Achievr** is a modern productivity application designed to help users stay focused, organized, and on track with their goals. Featuring a sleek calendar interface and intuitive task prioritization tools, Achievr empowers you to plan smarter and achieve more—one task at a time.

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Smart Calendar View**  
  Easily manage your schedule with a visually clean and interactive calendar.

- ✅ **Task Prioritization**  
  Organize tasks based on importance and urgency to boost daily efficiency.

- 🧠 **Minimalist Design**  
  Enjoy a modern, distraction-free interface that promotes clarity and focus.

- 🕒 **Daily Planning Tools**  
  Create, schedule, and review your daily goals with just a few clicks.

- 📊 **Progress Tracking**  
  Monitor your achievements and stay motivated with simple productivity insights.

## 🚀 Why Achievr?

Achievr isn’t just a to-do list—it’s your personal productivity partner. Stay focused. Stay sharp. Stay achieving.
